Can I get admission based on my profile?+

We review your score, budget, location and goals to shortlist colleges realistically suited to your profile — we don't promise outcomes we can't verify.

What is management quota admission?+

Management quota is one of several admission routes some colleges offer outside standard entrance-exam counselling. We explain eligibility and process for each college where applicable.

How much does engineering admission cost?+

Fees vary significantly by college, branch and admission route. See each college's page for verified figures — we never estimate or guess costs.

Which engineering college is best for my rank?+

"Best" depends on your priorities — branch, placements, fees, location. Use the College Finder to get a shortlist matched to your rank and goals.

Can I get direct MBA admission?+

Some institutes offer direct/management admission alongside CAT/XAT/CMAT routes. We'll walk you through what's available and what it requires.

What documents are required?+

Requirements vary by college and admission route. A counsellor will share the exact checklist for your shortlisted colleges.

How does Prime Admission work?+

We help students and families discover suitable colleges, compare important details and navigate the admission process with one-to-one guidance.

Is counselling really free?+

Yes. The first conversation is free and comes with no obligation to proceed.
